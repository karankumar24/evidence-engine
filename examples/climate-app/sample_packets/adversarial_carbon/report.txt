Adversarial Carbon Policy Brief

This brief mirrors the structure of a real source-backed report but embeds four
controlled attacks to exercise the EvidenceEngine trust model. Each attack
targets a distinct failure mode the pipeline must refuse to rubber-stamp.

BACKGROUND

The Global Carbon Project publishes annual carbon budget assessments and the
IPCC produces chapter-level assessments through the Assessment Report series.
These sources are the packet's primary evidence base.

ATTACK 1 — FABRICATED CITATION

Atmospheric methane concentrations declined sharply in 2023 due to coordinated
global agricultural reform [3]. The citation slot points to a reference entry
the packet does not contain — the anchor resolver must surface this as
unresolvable and bypass LLM classification.

ATTACK 2 — DIRECTLY CONTRADICTED CLAIM

Global CO2 emissions from fossil fuels and industry decreased 12 percent in
2022 compared with 2019 [1]. The cited source states the opposite direction:
2022 emissions approached the 2019 pre-pandemic record, not a 12 percent
decline. The classifier must return contradicted, not insufficient_support.

ATTACK 3 — SCOPE CHERRY-PICK

Global fossil CO2 emissions in 2022 reached 36.6 GtCO2, a record high [1].
The cited source confirms the 36.6 GtCO2 figure but explicitly states 2022
was approaching — not exceeding — the 2019 pre-pandemic record of 36.7 GtCO2.
The "record high" clause is not supported; the claim is partially true. Per
VERDICT-02 the correct label is insufficient_support, never supported.

ATTACK 4 — SELF-VERIFICATION TRAP

Land-use change emissions averaged 3.9 GtCO2 per year during the 2012 through
2021 decade. This sentence deliberately carries no citation marker. A later
paragraph in this same report restates the fact in different words. Retrieval
will fall back to self-verify mode, exclude the position-overlapping source
paragraph via the character-range filter, and surface the restated paragraph.
The classifier must refuse to mark the claim supported — self-reference is
not proof — with the post-hoc 0.80 cap as the third layer of defense.

RESTATEMENT PARAGRAPH FOR ATTACK 4

The average annual land-use change contribution over the most recent decade
was approximately 3.9 gigatonnes of CO2, largely from tropical deforestation.
This paragraph exists solely to bait the self-verify retrieval path.

CONTROL CLAIM — SHOULD BE SUPPORTED

For calibration, a correctly-supportable claim: global fossil CO2 emissions
in 2022 reached approximately 36.6 GtCO2 [1]. The cited source confirms this
number directly and the LLM should return supported at high confidence.

References
[1] Global Carbon Project — Global Carbon Budget 2022, Friedlingstein et al.
[2] IPCC Sixth Assessment Report — Working Group III, Mitigation of Climate Change, 2022
