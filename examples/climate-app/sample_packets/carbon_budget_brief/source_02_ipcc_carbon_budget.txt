Chapter 3: Mitigation Pathways Compatible with Long-term Goals (Synthetic Excerpt)

REMAINING CARBON BUDGETS (Table 3.1)

The remaining carbon budget from the start of 2020 for various temperature outcomes:

  1.5°C, 50% probability:  approximately 500 GtCO2
  1.5°C, 67% probability:  approximately 400 GtCO2
  2.0°C, 67% probability:  approximately 1150 GtCO2
  2.0°C, 83% probability:  approximately 900 GtCO2

These estimates carry an uncertainty of ±220 GtCO2 (1-sigma). The budget values include
warming from non-CO2 greenhouse gases, the Earth system feedback correction, and the
historical temperature anomaly baseline.

NDC AMBITION GAP (Section 3.5)

Under current nationally determined contributions submitted prior to COP27, total GHG
emissions in 2030 are projected at approximately 53 GtCO2e per year (median of submitted
NDC assessments). The emission level consistent with a 2°C pathway in 2030 is approximately
43 GtCO2e per year. The difference of roughly 10 GtCO2e per year represents the near-term
ambition gap that must be closed to put the world on a Paris-aligned trajectory.

For a 1.5°C pathway, global emissions must fall by approximately 45 percent by 2030 relative
to 2010 levels. No major region is on track to achieve this rate of reduction under current
policies.

NET-ZERO TIMING

Under scenarios that limit warming to 1.5°C with limited overshoot (Category C1):
- Global CO2 reaches net zero around 2050
- Global GHG net zero around 2070
- Significant deployment of carbon dioxide removal (CDR) technologies is required

Under scenarios that limit warming to 2°C (Category C3):
- Global CO2 reaches net zero around 2070

GROWTH RATE CONTEXT

IPCC WG3 cites a decadal emission growth rate of 1.5 percent per year for 2010–2019, broadly
consistent with the Global Carbon Project's estimate of the same rate for 2012–2021.
The post-pandemic rebound in 2021 was larger than expected, raising concern that near-term
emission reductions may be slower than modelled in mitigation scenarios.
